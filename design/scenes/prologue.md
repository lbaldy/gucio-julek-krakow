# Sen (`prologue`, cards)
Chapter: I · Connects to: town · Music: `legend` (reused) → stops at card 3 → a soft sting at card 5 · Ambience: per card

## Purpose
About 60–75 s. Mama's voice fades into the narrator's, and the boys fall asleep *into*
the legend. We meet Kłębuszek before he's taken, see the dragon only as eyes and a
tail, feel the empty fold, laugh at the fleeing knights, and land with the boys by the
Vistula. Feeling and turn: cosy → worry → a laugh → wonder.
