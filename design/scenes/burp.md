# Smok odlatuje (`burp`, cards)
Chapter: V · Connects to: den · Music: tension → comic plucks → **silence** → a big brass "BEEEK" sting → flight theme · Ambience: per card

## Purpose
About 45 s. The dragon's comic downfall, the legend's best-known moment, told mostly in
sound. Nobody is hurt: he gets a terrible tummy ache, drinks, burps, and flies away for
good. Feeling and turn: tension → laughter → relief.
