# Kupiec (`merchant`)

## Role
The market trader who sells stinky yellow sulphur stones nobody wants. **Arc (the town's
arc, with the baker):** laughs at Dratewka through the window → trades a stone for an
obwarzanek ("Na smoka?… Hm.") → peeks in at the sewing, impressed ("On naprawdę zrobił
owcę!") → cheers and lifts Dratewka in the outro.

Scenes: `town` (window, laughing), `market` (his stall), `workshop` (window, curious),
`outro` card 2 (painted).

## Personality
Grumpy, hungry, proud, secretly soft. Grumbles "Hmpf".

## Look (for art)
A stout middle-aged man, ≈ 1.3× Gucio's height, a round belly, a bushy black moustache,
thick eyebrows set in a frown, a felt cap, a mustard-yellow tunic with a brown belt and a
pouch. Poses: `idle` (arms crossed, behind the counter), `talk`, `eat` (biting the
obwarzanek, eyes closed in bliss), `window` (head and shoulders, laughing / curious: two frames).

## Voice brief
A grumpy, slightly gruff middle-aged Polish man (40s–50s), who warms up. Comic but natural.
Library audition first.

### Sample lines
- "Za darmo? Od rana tu stoję i nic nie jadłem… Za obwarzanka bym dał!"
- "Szewczyk na smoka? Ha ha ha!"
- "Patrz… on naprawdę zrobił owcę!"
