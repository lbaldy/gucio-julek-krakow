# Stary pasterz (`shepherd`)

## Role
The old shepherd who counts his sheep every night, and every morning has one fewer. His
favourite is the little lamb **Kłębuszek**. He is the heart of the stake: Gucio's promise is
made to him. **Arc:** sad and counting, holding the last tuft of Kłębuszek's wool → hugging
Kłębuszek: "Dotrzymałeś słowa, chłopcze." He gives Julek the tuft.

Scenes: `prologue` cards 1 and 3, `town` (the promise beat), `meadow` (gives the sheepskin,
hints at the ram), `outro` cards 1, 4 (and the bonus).

## Personality
Slow-talking, kind, a bit sad, with dry country humour that comes back at the end. Patient
with children; talks to his sheep like to grandchildren.

## Look (for art)
A small, stooped old grandpa, ≈ 1.2× Gucio's height. A wide frayed **straw hat**, a white
moustache and stubble, kind crinkled eyes. A long undyed linen shirt with a red woven belt,
a sleeveless sheepskin vest, rolled-up trousers, bast shoes. A long wooden **crook**. Usually
holds a small white **tuft of wool** in one hand.

Poses: `idle` (leaning on the crook, holding the tuft), `talk`, `sad` (looking at the tuft),
`happy` (arms open). Hugging Kłębuszek is painted in the outro card only.

## Voice brief
A slow, kind, slightly raspy Polish country grandpa (70s). Gentle, unhurried, warm, with soft
pauses. Not comic, not mumbling; clear for children. Library audition first.

## Speech style
Slow, short sentences with pauses ("…"). Calls the boys "dzieci", Gucio "chłopcze", Julek
"maluchu", the sheep "owieczki".

### Sample lines
- "…i Kłębuszek. Wszystkie. Dobranoc, owieczki."
- "Bodzio jest uparty. Lubi tylko mlecze… i maluchów nigdy nie bodzie."
- "Dotrzymałeś słowa, chłopcze."
