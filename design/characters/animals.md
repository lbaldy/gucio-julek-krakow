# Animals and props with a face

## Kłębuszek (`klebuszek`)
The old shepherd's smallest, whitest lamb, "jak piłeczka" (bouncy as a ball). The stake of
the whole game. Seen in `prologue` card 1 (hopping into the fold), in the `den` (curled in the
hollow, then standing beside Julek), and in `outro` cards 1, 4 and the bonus.
- **Look:** a tiny round lamb, half Julek's height, a fluffy cloud of curly white wool, a
  small black face with pink inner ears, big shiny eyes, a stubby tail. Poses: `curled`
  (asleep in a ball; in the hollow only the tail pokes out), `stand`.
- **Sound:** a small high "meee" (sfx, no voice).

## Bodzio the ram (`ram`)
The flock's grumpy ram, guarding the haystack on the meadow. Butts anyone big; never butts
little ones; loves dandelions. Named after "bodzie" (he butts).
- **Look:** a stocky ram with big curled horns, shaggy grey-cream wool, a long grumpy face
  with heavy eyebrows. Poses: `angry` (head down, front hoof scraping), `soft` (lying down,
  chewing a dandelion, eyes half-closed, a little smile).
- **Sound:** a grumpy low "beeeh", a butt "bonk" (reuse `boing`), a contented chew.

## The flock (`sheep`)
Ordinary white sheep: 3 sprite variants (grazing, standing, lying asleep), used on the meadow
and in the den. Bleats (sfx).

## The knight (`knight`)
One fleeing knight in `town`: a round helmet, a chainmail shirt over a red tunic, running with
arms flailing and an alarmed face. One pose (`run`), moved by a tween; the helmet is a separate
sprite (`helmet`) that bounces off. Clanking sfx. Comic, never hurt.
