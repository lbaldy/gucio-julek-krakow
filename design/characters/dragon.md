# Smok Wawelski (`dragon`)

## Role
The greedy dragon under Wawel hill. He doesn't eat the sheep: he **hoards them like treasure**
"na później" and counts them before sleep. He collects the knights' dropped helmets, too. He is
fooled by the fake sheep only once it bleats, gets a terrible tummy ache from the sulphur, drinks
half the Vistula, burps a gigantic BEEEK and flies away over the mountains for good.

Scenes: `prologue` card 2 (eyes and tail), `cave` (the sniff, doubt and gulp), `burp` cards 1–5.

## Personality
Big, greedy, silly, a spoiled big baby. Suspicious in a comic, slow way. Never shown hurting
anyone, never roaring at the boys. At the end, a bit embarrassed.

## Look (for art)
A big round green dragon, ≈ 2.5× Gucio's height when his head and chest are out of the cave.
Rounded picture-book shapes: a big belly in pale yellow scales, a long snout with big round
nostrils that glow orange and puff little smoke curls, round yellow eyes with heavy lids, small
blunt horns, a row of soft rounded back spikes, small comic wings, a long tail with a leaf-shaped
tip. Leaf-green with darker green spots, like a folk-art illustration. Friendly, not fierce.

Poses (in the cave mouth, head and front only): `sniff` (eyes shut, nostrils flared),
`doubt` (one eye squinting, eyebrow up), `happy` (eyes shut, mouth open, delighted).
Everything else is in the cards.

## Voice brief (voice design)
A deep, grumbly, silly Polish dragon voice: big and rumbly but childish and greedy, like a
huge spoiled toddler, with slow suspicious "hmmm"s and greedy "mniam". Warm and comic, never
menacing or scary. Few lines; short words.

## Speech style
Two to four words at a time. Possessive ("Moja! Moje!"), sniffy, slow.

### Sample lines
- "Owieczki… Moje!"
- "Owca… która nie beczy? Hmmm…"
- "Mniam… Oj. Ojojoj… Brzuch!"
