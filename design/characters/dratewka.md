# Szewczyk Dratewka (`dratewka`)

## Role
A young shoemaker with the clever plan nobody believes. His name comes from **dratwa**, the
strong waxed thread shoemakers sew with (he says so himself). **Arc:** laughed at ("Szewczyk
na smoka? Ha ha!") and a bit hurt → "Naprawdę? … No to witajcie!" when the boys believe him →
shaking at the cave but staying ("Wy jesteście dzielni, to i ja będę.") → the town's hero, who
says he couldn't have done it alone.

Scenes: `workshop` (plan, needle, dratwa, assembly), `cave` (the cart, the peg), `den` (the
lantern), `outro` card 2.

## Personality
Kind, quick, a bit clumsy, bursting with ideas; talks fast when excited, whispers when scared.
Generous with praise ("Maluchu, jesteś skarbem!").

## Look (for art)
A slim young man, ≈ 1.3× Gucio's height, round friendly face, freckles, a mop of curly
chestnut hair, big bright eyes. A cream linen shirt with rolled sleeves, a brown **leather
apron** with pockets (an awl and pegs sticking out), a red neckerchief, simple trousers, worn
leather shoes he made himself (one with a visible patch).

Poses: `idle`, `talk` (×2, one with a raised finger: the idea), `sew` (at the bench, needle
high), `scared` (holding a lantern, knees together), `cheer` (arms up).

## Voice brief
A young, bright, friendly Polish man (20s), slightly nervous energy, quick and warm, able to
whisper well. Not a cartoon voice. Library audition first.

## Speech style
Quick, exclamatory, practical. Lists things on his fingers. Calls the boys "chłopaki" or
"dzieciaki", Julek "maluchu".

### Sample lines
- "Zrobimy owcę, która nie jest owcą!"
- "Szewc zawsze ma kołek!"
- "Sam bym nie dał rady. To oni!"
