# Mama (`mama`)

> **Series character.** Voice carried over (`assets/voices.json`). No sprite: she is
> off-screen, as in the rest of the series.

## Role
She frames the legend. At bedtime she reads it (the `book_page` card shows her hands holding
the book) and stops at the cliffhanger: "Dokończymy jutro." Her voice fades into the narrator's
as the boys fall asleep. In the morning she comes to finish the legend, finds the boys already
know the ending, notices the wool, and promises a holiday trip to Kraków to see the real dragon.

## Speech style
Warm, a little teasing, calls the boys "owieczki" (after Julek's game), "chłopcy".

### Sample lines
- "Julek! Owieczki nie skaczą po łóżeczku!"
- "Dokończymy jutro."
- "W wakacje pojedziemy do Krakowa. Zobaczycie prawdziwego smoka."
