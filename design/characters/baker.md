# Piekarka (`baker`) and the baby (`baby`)

## Role
The baker with a cradle at her stall. Her baby won't stop crying, so she can't bake. Julek's
"Kuku!" makes the baby laugh, and she gives the boys an obwarzanek. She shares the town's arc
with the merchant: laughing → curious → cheering.

Scenes: `town` (window, laughing), `market` (her stall), `workshop` (window, curious),
`outro` card 2 (painted).

## Personality
Warm, busy, tired, quick to laugh. Grateful.

## Look (for art)
A young woman, ≈ 1.2× Gucio's height, rosy round face, a blond braid under a white headscarf
tied at the back, a flour-dusted apron over a red skirt with a folk border, sleeves rolled up.
Poses: `idle` (rocking the cradle), `talk`, `give` (holding out an obwarzanek), `window`
(head and shoulders, laughing / curious).

**The baby:** only seen in the cradle, swaddled in a folk-patterned blanket: `baby_cry`
(scrunched red face, mouth open) and `baby_laugh` (beaming, tiny hands up).

## Voice brief
A warm, slightly frazzled young Polish woman (late 20s), friendly and bright. Must be clearly
different from Mama's voice. Library audition first. The baby: crying and giggling sfx.

### Sample lines
- "Mój maluszek płacze i płacze. Nie mogę piec!"
- "Cisza! Śmieje się! Masz, maluchu, obwarzanek za to!"
